Last updated: 16 November 2026

ByzForm (“we”, “Team ByzForm”) is a service in its pilot phase that monitors public IT tenders published on TED (Tenders Electronic Daily) and sends companies pre-qualified summaries. By requesting or using the digest, Scouting or a Bid Pack you agree to these terms. Contact: byzform@byzform.com.

1. Pilot phase

The service is offered as a pilot. Features, formats and delivery times may change as we improve it. Pilot clients receive a 50% discount for their first 3 months of Scouting and on Bid Packs ordered during that period.

2. Services

  • Weekly Digest – free, sent by email, can be cancelled any time by replying “stop”.
  • Scouting – daily digest of new matching tenders; includes a free 2-week trial.
  • Bid Pack – structured analysis of one tender’s documentation (requirements matrix, checklist, clarification questions, draft technical section), delivered by email, normally within 48 hours of receiving the full documentation.

3. Ordering and payment

All paid services are paid in advance by invoice and bank transfer; no payment card is collected. Scouting is a monthly subscription: after the free trial, each month is paid in advance. A Bid Pack is paid in advance when ordered. Pilot clients get 50% off Scouting for the first 3 months. Prices are in EUR and exclude VAT. Scouting can be cancelled at any time with effect from the next month; there is no minimum term.

Summaries, fit scores and bid/no-bid verdicts are our assessment based on public notices and the profile you give us. They are informational and do not replace your own review of the official tender documents. Deadlines, requirements and values must always be checked against the original notice on TED or the buyer’s platform. You remain solely responsible for the decision to bid, the content of your bid, pricing, legal declarations and submission.
