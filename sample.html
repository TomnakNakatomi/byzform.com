1. Electronic records management system - Prague Public Transit Company

Original title Elektronický systém spisové služby (Czech)
Buyer Dopravní podnik hl. m. Prahy, akciová společnost - Prague, Czechia
Estimated value CZK 35,500,000 excl. VAT ≈ €1.45M
Submission deadline 17 Dec 2026, 10:00 CET
Procedure Open
Contract duration 80 months
TED 795261-2026

What they want - Supply, implementation, operational support and development of an electronic records management system (eSSL). - Compliance with Czech archiving law 499/2004 Coll., the National Standard for eSSL and the attestation requirements. - Migration of data and documents from the current AthenA system, keeping integrity, links and metadata.

Award criteria: bid price and the delivery date of Stage 1.

Watch out: the product must be attested under Czech law, so this suits vendors with their own eSSL or a partnership with one.

2. New “Air Quality” portal - Chief Inspectorate of Environmental Protection (Poland)

Original title Przygotowanie i wdrożenie nowego Portalu “Jakość Powietrza” GIOŚ wraz z usługami API oraz dostosowaniem aplikacji mobilnej (Polish)
Buyer Główny Inspektorat Ochrony Środowiska - Warsaw, Poland
Estimated value PLN 3,577,235.78 ≈ €0.80M
Submission deadline 14 Dec 2026, 10:00 CET
Procedure Open
TED 799087-2026

What they want - Design and launch a new national “Air Quality” portal with API services. - Adapt the existing mobile app “Jakość Powietrza w Polsce” to the new portal. - EU-funded (FEnIKS 2021-2027), so the “do no significant harm” (DNSH) principle applies.

Award criteria: price plus points for the experience of the project manager, system architect, requirements analyst and UX designer, and for the warranty period.

Watch out: quality points depend on named key staff, so CVs and references decide a large share of the score.

3. Traffic-responsive signal control - City of České Budějovice

Original title Nadstavbové řízení SSZ v Českých Budějovicích - dopravně závislé řízení (Czech)
Buyer Statutární město České Budějovice - Czechia
Estimated value CZK 31,116,400 excl. VAT ≈ €1.27M
Submission deadline 5 Jan 2027, 13:00 CET
Procedure Open
Contract duration 138 months
TED 805809-2026

What they want - Software changes, project documentation and implementation of a higher-level control layer for 41 traffic signal controllers in 6 locations. - Real-time, traffic-responsive control that reacts to demand, weather, accidents and closures. - Design of control algorithms and signal plan changes, plus a traffic simulation with an ex-ante quality assessment.

Award criteria: total price, reference projects and the proposed solution.

Watch out: it must fit the architecture of the existing traffic control system, so domain references in traffic engineering will matter.

4. Support for health information systems ISZI and MIS - NCZI (Slovakia)

Original title Poskytovanie podporných služieb pre zabezpečenie prevádzky informačných systémov ISZI a MIS NCZI (Slovak)
Buyer Národné centrum zdravotníckych informácií - Bratislava, Slovakia
Estimated value €3,269,323.56 excl. VAT
Submission deadline 22 Dec 2026, 09:00 CET
Procedure Open
Contract duration 36 months
TED 808784-2026

What they want - Technical support, maintenance and development of the Health Indicators Information System (ISZI) and the NCZI Management Information System (MIS). - Flat-fee services under agreed service levels, plus ordered services: change requests, testing, implementation and migration work.

Award criteria: lowest price only.

Watch out: price-only award on an existing system usually favours the incumbent or a team that already knows the code base.

5. EDR/XDR and SIEM with MDR - University Hospital Plzeň

Original title Pořízení a implementace integrovaného řešení EDR/XDR a SIEM (Czech)
Buyer Fakultní nemocnice Plzeň - Czechia
Estimated value CZK 76,000,000 excl. VAT ≈ €3.11M
Submission deadline 23 Dec 2026, 09:00 CET
Procedure Open
Contract duration 68 months
TED 813679-2026

What they want - Supply, implementation and support of endpoint and server protection with incident response (EDR/XDR) and an integrated SIEM. - Managed Detection & Response (MDR) service for 60 months. - Current-state analysis, Solution Design document, licences, integration, automation and training.

Award criteria: bid price only.

Watch out: a 60-month MDR service means you need a 24/7 SOC, your own or a partner’s.
