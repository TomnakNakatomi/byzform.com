Questions

Where does the data come from?

From TED (Tenders Electronic Daily), the EU's official journal of public procurement, which publishes every above-threshold tender in the EU and EEA. We read the original notice and link to it, so you can always check the source.

How is this different from a tender alert service?

Alerts match keywords and leave you a long list. We read each notice against your actual capabilities and tell you whether it's worth your bid team's time - including the reasons it isn't.

Which languages and countries?

All 27 EU countries plus EEA. Notices are read in their original language and summarised in English (or Czech, Slovak, Polish or German on request).

Do you write the whole bid?

No. The Bid Pack gives your team a structured head start - requirements, checklist, questions and a draft technical section. Pricing, legal declarations and the final submission stay with you.

How do I pay?

During the pilot nothing is paid upfront. If you decide to continue after your free trial, or once your Bid Pack is delivered and you are happy with it, we email you the payment details (EUR bank transfer). Scouting can be cancelled any time.

What does “pilot” mean?

ByzForm is new. Pilot clients get the full service at half price for the first 3 months in exchange for honest feedback. We may adjust formats and features as we learn.
