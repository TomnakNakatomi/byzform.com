What the documents hide (priced in)

  1. Licences must run 60 months from the final acceptance, and the rollout may take up to 12 months. Price up to 72 months of vendor subscription into the delivery phase.
  2. The 400 devices are only a minimum. The buyer confirmed more than 10,000 networked devices must be analysed and cannot be excluded from licensing.
  3. Data collection is required from 6 places (new and old server room, each 2x10G SFP+ and 4x1G, plus 4 remote sites). Site details are withheld until after award.
  4. Integration with all three scanners (Tenable, Rapid7, Qualys) and a direct Check Point integration in two modes. Versions of Aruba, ClearPass and PACS are not disclosed.
  5. All virtual components together may use at most 32 cores and 128 GB RAM. Event and inventory data must be kept at least 18 months, no packet capture. Cloud metadata is allowed only in the EU (SOC 2 accepted).
  6. Penalties: 0.05% of price per day of delay, CZK 1,000 per day per defect (up to 50% or 80% of price), CZK 500 per started hour for a critical incident (start in 4 working hours, fix in 24 h).
  7. No demo or refurbished equipment, no variants, a single lot, and any single "NO" in the requirements table means exclusion.

Questions worth sending to the buyer

  • What device count and growth should the licence price assume above 10,000?
  • Which of the three scanner integrations must be demonstrated at acceptance?
  • How do delay penalties apply when the buyer provides network mirroring (SPAN) and access? Latest date to ask: about 9 Dec 2026 (8 working days before the deadline).
