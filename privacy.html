Privacy Notice

Version dated 23 November 2026

This notice explains how Team ByzForm handles personal data. Contact Team ByzForm at byzform@byzform.com with questions or requests about your data.

What we process

  • Business contact details: name, work email, company, role.
  • Company profile you send us (services, countries, contract sizes).
  • Email correspondence with you.

Public tender data from TED may contain names of contact persons at contracting authorities; we only reproduce what is published there.

Why and on what basis

  • To send you the digest or services you asked for - performance of a contract / steps before a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To contact companies with an offer relevant to their business, using publicly available business contact details - our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Every such email offers a simple way to opt out, and we stop immediately if you reply “stop” or object.

Who receives the data

We use Seznam.cz for email and GitHub Pages to host the website. The language selector loads flag images from FlagCDN, which receives a request from your browser to deliver them. The website has no contact form or ByzForm analytics, cookies or tracking scripts. We do not sell your personal data.

How long

For as long as you use the service and up to 12 months afterwards; prospect contact data is deleted within 12 months if there is no response, or immediately on objection.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to processing at any time by emailing byzform@byzform.com. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in the Czech Republic: Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, uoou.gov.cz).
